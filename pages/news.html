Article about SemantyFish at Eurofish magazine

We are pleased to announce the publication of a new article on SemantyFish in Eurofish Magazine, Issue 6 2025 (November-December). The article, titled Connecting fish data for open and sustainable science presents the main objectives, methodological innovations, and expected impact of SemantyFish. It highlights how the project is transforming FIshBase into an interoperable knowledge graph, enaabling richer biodiversity research, advanced data integration, and stronger support for open science and sustainability.

Eurofish magazine is a widely read international publication covering developments in fisheries, aquaculture, processing, and emerging technologies across the sector. The inclusion of SemantyFish in this issue marks an important milestone in communicating the project's vision and progress to a broad global audience. You can read the article at https://eurofish.dk/magazine-issues/em-6-2025/ (pages 48-49)

October 1, 2024

SemantyFish officially launched

SemantyFish aims to enhance the visibility, interoperability, and exploitability of FishBase by transforming it into a semantic web knowledge base. By leveraging semantic web technologies, the project will enable seamless data exchange, complex queries, and integration with global research infrastructures, advancing its role in open science and interdisciplinary research. SemantyFish is an open science project funded under the OSCARS 1st Open Call for Open Science Projects and Services. Starting in October 2024, the Foundation for Research and Technology-Hellas (FORTH) and Quantitative Aquatics Inc. (Q-quatics) have joined forces for the next 24 months to implement SemantyFish. The official Press Release can be found here.
